# SAP ERP vs. SAP S/4HANA – Logistics Process Comparison

## 1. Purpose

This document compares the classic SAP ERP processes covered in my university coursework with selected concepts of SAP S/4HANA.

The original practical exercises were performed in a classic SAP ERP environment. This document is a learning and comparison exercise; it does not claim that the described processes have been executed in a live SAP S/4HANA system.

## 2. Process Overview

The university case study covered an end-to-end business process for the production and sale of hardware products.

The process included:

1. Customer inquiry and quotation
2. Sales order creation
3. Material requirements planning (MRP)
4. Procurement of missing components
5. Goods receipt and inventory management
6. Production order processing
7. Delivery and goods issue
8. Billing and incoming payment

The case study demonstrated how sales, procurement, production, warehouse operations and financial accounting interact within an integrated ERP process.

These transaction codes document the classic ERP learning environment. They should not be assumed to be the recommended user interface for every equivalent process in SAP S/4HANA.

## 4. Selected SAP S/4HANA Concepts

### 4.1 Modern User Experience

SAP S/4HANA supports SAP Fiori as a modern user experience, including role-based applications. The user interface and way of accessing a business process can therefore differ from the classic SAP GUI transactions used in the university exercises.

### 4.2 Business Partner

In classic SAP ERP, customer and supplier master data were commonly maintained through separate customer and vendor transactions in the training scenario.

SAP S/4HANA uses the Business Partner approach as the central entry point for maintaining business partner data, including customer and supplier roles.

### 4.3 Integrated Business Processes

The core business logic remains process-oriented: sales demand can trigger planning, procurement or production; goods movements update inventory; and delivery and billing connect logistics with financial processes.

The exact applications, configuration and process steps depend on the SAP S/4HANA edition and the business scenario.

### 4.4 Material Requirements Planning

The university case used MRP to identify material requirements and create planning proposals. In SAP S/4HANA, material requirements planning remains an important capability, but the specific applications and execution options depend on the system configuration and release.

## 5. Master Data and Transaction Data

The case study distinguished between master data and business transactions.

**Master data** included materials, customers, suppliers, bills of material, routings and purchasing information records.

**Transaction data** was generated by business activities such as sales orders, purchase orders, goods movements, production orders, deliveries and billing documents.

Reliable master data is essential for consistent execution of integrated business processes.
